Strait Command

Four island nations share one sea. Build your naval base, train sailors to crew your ships and aircraft, and fight for control of the water. Every alliance is temporary.

  • Build shipyards, airfields, coastal guns and flak batteries
  • Every ship and aircraft needs a crew trained at your barracks
  • Diplomacy with real politics: elections, coups and allies who turn
  • Seize oil rigs for fuel and steel, sack an enemy HQ for its treasury
  • Win by being the last flag flying
