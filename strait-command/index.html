Strait Command

Four island nations share one sea. Build your naval base, crew your fleet, pick your friends carefully, and be the last flag standing.

  • Funds, steel and fuel come from your HQ, Supply Depots and Fuel Depots. Everything costs a mix of all three.
  • Sailors are recruited at Barracks. Every ship and aircraft needs a crew, and idle sailors walk over to board it.
  • Command: tap a ship or aircraft, then tap where it should go. Units fire on anything hostile in range. Jets are hard to hit; build Flak Batteries to bring them down.
  • Nations: propose alliances, send aid and watch the relations meters. Elections, coups and incidents shift loyalties, and an ally can turn on you.
  • Win by being the last nation standing. A nation falls when its base is levelled and a hostile ship lands to plant a flag, or when it is wiped out and broke.

Drag to pan, pinch to zoom, tap the minimap to jump. Landscape works best.
